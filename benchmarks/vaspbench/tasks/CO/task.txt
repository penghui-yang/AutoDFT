Use density-functional theory to find the equilibrium C–O bond length of an isolated carbon monoxide (CO) molecule by allowing the two atoms to move until the forces vanish and the total energy is minimized. CO is a simple but chemically important polar diatomic molecule with a strong multiple bond, making it a good benchmark for how well the chosen electronic-structure method reproduces molecular geometry. The system is treated as a single molecule in a large empty box so that it behaves like a gas-phase molecule rather than interacting with periodic images. The expected output is the optimized C–O bond distance (and corresponding minimum total energy), which can be compared directly to experimental bond lengths or used as a reference for studying CO adsorption and reactions on surfaces.
