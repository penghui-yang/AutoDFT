Compute the electronic density of states (DOS) of silicon in the face‑centered‑cubic (fcc) crystal structure to see how many electronic states are available at each energy and whether the material is metallic or has a band gap. Although silicon is most stable in the diamond structure, the fcc phase is a useful reference for understanding how changing crystal packing alters bonding and electronic structure. The expected output is a DOS curve referenced to the Fermi level, from which you can identify the presence/size of any gap and the energy ranges where valence and conduction states occur. Typically, the DOS is obtained by first finding a well‑converged ground‑state electron density and then evaluating the DOS on a denser sampling of the Brillouin zone using that fixed ground state.
