Determine the equilibrium crystal structure of cubic diamond silicon by letting both the atomic positions and the lattice itself (cell volume and shape) relax until the internal forces and residual stress are minimized. This calculation targets the ground‑state lattice constant and equilibrium volume of bulk Si—basic but important reference quantities that set the correct density and bonding geometry for predicting many other properties (elastic constants, phonons, defect energies, etc.). The key expected result is the relaxed lattice parameter (about 5.47 Å for this setup) together with a near‑zero external pressure/stress state, indicating the structure is at mechanical equilibrium. In practice, the equilibrium volume can be obtained either directly from a full relaxation or more robustly by computing total energy at several volumes and fitting an equation of state, which also helps diagnose small numerical stresses that can bias the optimized volume.
