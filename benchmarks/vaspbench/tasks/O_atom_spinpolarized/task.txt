Compute the ground-state electronic structure of an isolated oxygen atom placed in a large, empty periodic box, with electron spin treated explicitly. Oxygen is an open‑shell atom with two unpaired valence electrons, so it should carry a nonzero magnetic moment; this makes it a simple but important test case for whether a spin-polarized DFT setup can correctly describe magnetism. The key expected result is a total magnetic moment of about \(2\,\mu_B\), reflecting two more electrons in one spin channel than the other. You should also obtain separate energy levels and occupations for the spin‑up and spin‑down electrons, illustrating how exchange splitting produces the atom’s net magnetization.
