Carry out a first-principles calculation of bulk nickel oxide (NiO) in its antiferromagnetic ground state, where neighboring Ni atoms carry opposite spins. NiO is a classic “strongly correlated” transition‑metal oxide: the Ni 3d electrons are too localized for plain DFT to describe accurately, so a Hubbard‑U correction is used to capture the correct electron localization, magnetic moments, and insulating character. The main expected outputs are the converged total energy for the antiferromagnetic state, the site‑resolved magnetic moments on Ni, and the Ni 3d orbital occupations (occupation matrices) that reveal how the d electrons are distributed among spin and orbital channels. You would typically also examine the density of states to confirm the presence of an insulating gap and the Ni‑d character of the valence and conduction edges.
