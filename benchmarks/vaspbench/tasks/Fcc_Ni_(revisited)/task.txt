Compute the **spin-resolved, orbital‑projected electronic density of states (partial DOS)** of **bulk face‑centered‑cubic (fcc) nickel**, a classic **itinerant ferromagnetic metal**. The goal is to quantify how Ni’s electronic states near the Fermi level split into **majority (spin‑up) and minority (spin‑down)** channels and how much of that DOS comes from different orbital characters (especially the Ni \(d\) states), which underpins its magnetism and metallic behavior. The key results should be a **spin‑up and spin‑down DOS/partial DOS** showing a **finite magnetic moment per Ni atom (~0.6 \(\mu_B\))** and an **exchange splitting on the order of ~0.5 eV** between the two spin channels. Because ferromagnets can converge to either magnetic or nearly nonmagnetic solutions depending on how the magnetism is initialized, the calculation is also a check that the **correct ferromagnetic ground state** has been obtained.
