Compute the carbon **K-edge X-ray absorption near-edge structure (XANES)** of **bulk diamond** to predict how diamond absorbs X-rays when a **1s (core) electron** is excited into the **unoccupied conduction-band states**. Because the presence of a core hole strongly perturbs the local electronic structure, the calculation explicitly includes a **core-excited atom** embedded in a large periodic diamond supercell so the artificial interaction between repeated core holes is minimized and the spectrum approaches the isolated-excitation limit. The key output is the **energy-dependent absorption spectrum**, typically obtained from the **imaginary part of the frequency-dependent dielectric response**, showing the near-edge peak positions and intensities that can be compared directly to experimental XANES. Convergence should be assessed by increasing the supercell size until the spectral features no longer change appreciably.
