Determine the **equilibrium lattice constant (and corresponding equilibrium volume)** of silicon constrained to the **face-centered-cubic (fcc)** crystal structure by computing the total energy for a series of slightly expanded and compressed unit cells. Although bulk Si is normally diamond-cubic, treating it in fcc provides a clean, one-atom test system that is useful for learning how DFT predicts structural stability and for benchmarking numerical settings. The expected result is an **energy–lattice-parameter curve** with a clear minimum; fitting this curve to an equation of state gives the **best lattice constant**, which in this example lies near **3.9 Å**. The converged ground-state electron density from the minimum-energy structure is typically kept for follow-on calculations that require a well-converged starting point.
