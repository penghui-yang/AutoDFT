Perform a geometry optimization of an isolated water (H₂O) molecule to find its lowest-energy gas-phase structure, i.e., the equilibrium O–H bond lengths and H–O–H bond angle where the atomic forces vanish. The molecule is placed in a large “empty” simulation box so it behaves like a single, non‑interacting molecule rather than a condensed phase, which is important for comparing to molecular spectroscopy or gas-phase thermochemistry. The expected outcome is the relaxed atomic geometry together with the corresponding total energy, which can be used as a reference for studying hydrogen bonding, adsorption of water on surfaces, or reaction energetics involving water.
