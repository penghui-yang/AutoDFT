This calculation finds the equilibrium bond length of an isolated oxygen molecule (O₂) by allowing the two atoms to move until the total energy is minimized and the interatomic forces vanish. O₂ is a chemically important diatomic molecule and is also *magnetic* in its ground state (it has unpaired electrons), so the calculation must treat the electron spin correctly to capture the proper bonding and energy. The molecule is placed in a large empty “box” so it behaves like a single, non‑interacting dimer rather than a periodic solid. The expected output is the optimized O–O distance (and the corresponding minimum total energy and magnetic moment) describing the stable molecular geometry.
