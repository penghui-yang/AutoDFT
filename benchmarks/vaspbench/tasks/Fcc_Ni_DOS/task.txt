Compute the electronic density of states (DOS) of bulk face‑centered‑cubic nickel to see how many electron states are available at each energy and, in particular, how many lie at the Fermi level where electrical conduction occurs. Nickel is a ferromagnetic transition metal, so the DOS is expected to be spin‑polarized (different for majority and minority spins), reflecting exchange splitting of the Ni \(3d\) bands. The main result should be a DOS plot referenced to the Fermi energy, from which you can identify the distribution of \(d\)- and \(s\)-derived states, the degree of spin imbalance, and whether the material remains metallic (finite DOS at the Fermi level).
