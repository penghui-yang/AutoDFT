Compute the effective on-site Coulomb interaction strength **U** for the **Ni 3d electrons** in **antiferromagnetic NiO**, a classic “strongly correlated” transition‑metal oxide where standard DFT often misrepresents the localization of d electrons, the band gap, and magnetic moments. Using the **Cococcioni linear‑response approach**, you slightly perturb the d-electron energy on one selected Ni atom in a **2×2×2 AFM-II supercell** (chosen so the two oppositely polarized Ni sublattices are represented and one Ni site can be treated as distinct) and monitor how the **d-electron occupancy** on that site changes. You perform this both with the electron density held fixed and with the system allowed to self-consistently screen the perturbation, so that the difference between “bare” and “screened” responses isolates the true interaction strength. The expected outcome is a **numerical value of U (in eV) for Ni d states in NiO**, which can then be used in subsequent DFT+U calculations to obtain more realistic electronic structure and magnetism.
