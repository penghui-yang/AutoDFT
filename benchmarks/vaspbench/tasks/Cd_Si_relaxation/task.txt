This calculation relaxes (energy-minimizes) the atomic positions in cubic diamond silicon after a small, deliberate distortion is introduced that breaks the perfect crystal symmetry (one atom is slightly displaced from its ideal diamond-site position). The physical goal is to find the equilibrium internal coordinates that restore zero net forces on the atoms, revealing how strongly the diamond Si lattice “pushes back” against a local perturbation. Silicon in the diamond structure is a prototypical covalent semiconductor, so the relaxed geometry reflects the balance of directional bonding and lattice symmetry. The expected result is a final set of atomic positions and the associated total energy/forces, with forces reduced to near zero, showing whether the displaced atom returns close to the ideal diamond configuration.
